# Signal SEO Guidelines

Target content:

- Commercial informational articles for electronics buyers in Moscow and Russia.
- 900-1600 words for comparison and buying-guide articles, matching existing Signal blog style.
- Primary keyword in title, intro, at least one H2, meta title, meta description, and slug.
- 4-7 H2 sections.
- 3-6 internal Signal links per article.
- No duplicate topics already present on Signal blog.
- Product recommendations must match live Signal inventory where possible.

Metadata:

- `metaTitle`: 50-65 characters when possible.
- `metaDescription`: 130-170 characters.
- Slugs: lowercase transliteration, hyphenated.
- Tags: 4-6 concise tags.
- Keywords: primary plus 3-6 secondary phrases.

Style constraints:

- No copied competitor text.
- No artificial filler.
- No unsupported exact technical claims when the product line changes quickly.
- Prefer scenario-based advice: camera, battery, travel, sport, work, ecosystem, budget.
