## SEO execution rules for this batch

Each article starts with the buying problem, gives a short answer early, then expands into scenario comparison, product-fit logic, Signal links and FAQ. The commercial block does not push the most expensive product by default; it explains when the higher model is justified and when the cheaper option is cleaner. This follows the seomachine rule used in this project: article quality is judged by intent satisfaction and next-step clarity, not by keyword stuffing.
